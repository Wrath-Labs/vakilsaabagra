About Rajeev Upadhyay

My work is commercial rather than courtroom-first: structuring a business properly, writing the contract that governs it, and keeping the filings clean so that none of it becomes litigation. Agra's client base is specific — shoe manufacturers and their component suppliers, exporters, engineering units, hotels on Fatehabad Road, and a growing number of first-time founders — and the legal problems it runs into are specific too.

Most of them come from documents that were never written down. A partnership with no deed, a distribution arrangement on a WhatsApp thread, an export order with no jurisdiction or arbitration clause, a founder who left with a third of the equity and no vesting. So the bulk of my practice is drafting and vetting: vendor and job-work agreements, distribution and agency contracts, employment and non-disclosure terms, founders' and shareholders' agreements, leave and licence deeds. Alongside that runs company and LLP incorporation, ROC and annual compliance, and trademark filing and opposition, which matters more than owners expect in an industry where a brand name is the asset.

When something has already gone wrong, I appear in commercial arbitrations and recovery matters arising out of supply contracts, before the Commercial Court at Agra, the NCLT at Allahabad and the Allahabad High Court. Being qualified as a Company Secretary as well as an advocate means the compliance side and the litigation side are looked at together — which usually shortens the argument about what the parties actually agreed to. I bill commercial work on a scoped basis, agreed in writing before it starts.
