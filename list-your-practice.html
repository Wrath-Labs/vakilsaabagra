Get listed on Vakil Saab Agra

No forms, no portal, no login. Send us one WhatsApp message with your details and we will verify and publish your profile — usually within two working days. What you pay is a fixed technology and listing service fee, billed in advance: never a commission, never a referral fee, and never any share of what a client pays you.

Step one

What to send us

Copy the message below into WhatsApp and fill in your details. Everything here appears on your public profile, so send it exactly as you would like it published.

Required

  • Full name as enrolled with the Bar Council
  • Bar Council enrolment number (e.g. UP/01234/2010)
  • Years of practice and the year you enrolled
  • Practice areas and case types you actually take on
  • Chamber address in Agra and the area name
  • Contact number clients may call directly

Helpful, but optional

  • A clear photograph — formal, plain background
  • Courts and forums where you regularly appear
  • Languages you can conduct a consultation in
  • Consultation fee and your usual chamber timings
  • Education, LL.M. or specialisations
  • A short paragraph about your practice, in your own words

Step two

What happens next

  1. 1

    We verify

    We confirm your enrolment number against Bar Council records and call you on the number you sent.

  2. 2

    You approve the draft

    We send you the profile text on WhatsApp. Nothing goes live until you confirm every detail is correct.

  3. 3

    It goes live

    Your profile is published with your own phone number. Clients call you directly — we never sit in between.

Step three

What it includes, and what it costs

A fixed technology and listing service fee, billed in advance for the period you choose. It pays for building and hosting the listing, keeping the details current and the site online. It is not a charge per enquiry, and it is never a share of your professional fee.

The directory listing

  • A full profile page with your own phone number on it — enrolment, experience, courts, languages, chamber address and consultation fee
  • Listed under every practice area and case type you actually take on, so people searching that problem find you
  • Findable by locality, experience, language and fee in the directory filters
  • Readable in Hindi and English
  • A monthly count of how many people opened your profile and how many tapped call or WhatsApp — sent to you, so you can judge for yourself whether it is worth renewing
  • Changes made on request; removal honoured within one working day

Your own microsite optional

  • A separate website on your own address, such as yourname.vakilsaabagra.in, with an SSL certificate
  • A longer account of your practice in your own words — not a copy of your directory profile
  • Your practice areas set out section by section, with the matters you handle under each
  • Answers to the questions your clients actually ask, in your own words
  • Call and WhatsApp always within reach on a phone, and a link that looks like yours when you send it
  • A choice of four looks, so it does not read like everybody else's page
