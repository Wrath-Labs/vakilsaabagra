Fees and costs

A first consultation in Agra typically ranges from about ₹400 to ₹2,000, depending on the advocate’s seniority and how complex the matter is. Case fees are separate and agreed between you and the advocate. Every profile on this site shows the consultation fee upfront.

Never. We do not take a percentage, a referral fee or a booking fee. Whatever you agree with the advocate is paid entirely to the advocate.

Yes. The District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) at Agra provides free legal aid to those who qualify, including women, children, Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe applicants, and people below the prescribed income limit.
