What we are, for advocates who list with us

Vakil Saab Agra is a technology and listing service. We build, host and maintain a listing — and, where taken, a microsite — in return for a fixed service fee agreed in advance. We are not a law firm, we do not practise law, and we are not an intermediary between an advocate and a client. We do not refer or recommend any advocate to any person, and we take no share, commission, referral fee or other part of any fee an advocate charges a client. Nothing an advocate pays us is calculated by reference to enquiries received or work obtained.

Listing fees, and what they do not promise

The service fee is payable in advance for the period chosen — monthly, half-yearly or yearly — and the current figures are sent in writing before any payment is taken. The fee pays for building and publishing the listing, keeping it available and keeping the site running.

We do not guarantee any number of enquiries, calls, clients or instructions, and we make no representation that a listing will produce work. The counts we report to an advocate — profile views and contact taps — describe what happened; they are not a forecast and not a promise. An advocate who needs a guaranteed volume of work should not buy a listing.

Cancellation and refunds

An advocate may ask for their listing to be taken down at any time, and we act on that within one working day.

Monthly plan. Cancelling stops the next month's billing. The current month is not refunded, and the listing runs to the end of it.

Six-month and yearly plans. Asked for within seven days of the listing going live, the whole fee is refunded. After seven days we refund the unused whole months, calculated at the monthly rate rather than the discounted rate that was prepaid, and not counting any month already begun — the discount is what the longer commitment bought, so it does not survive that commitment being cut short. A microsite is refunded on the same basis. Refunds go back the way the payment came, within fourteen working days.

We may end a listing ourselves, with a pro-rata refund of the unused whole months, if the enrolment details cannot be verified, if the advocate asks us to publish something we consider misleading or outside what the Bar Council of India Rules permit, or if the advocate no longer practises at Agra.

What happens if a listing is not renewed

A listing runs until the date it is paid up to. If it is not renewed by then, the profile is removed from the directory and from the sitemap, and any microsite on its own subdomain stops serving — the address will return a not-found page rather than the site. Nothing is deleted at our end: the record is kept, and renewing brings the profile and the microsite back at the same address exactly as they were.

We will normally remind an advocate before the date, but the responsibility for renewing is the advocate's, and a listing may go offline on expiry without further notice.
