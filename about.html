A lawyer directory built for Agra, and nowhere else

Vakil Saab Agra exists to answer one question well: who is the right advocate for my matter, here in Agra, and how do I reach them today?

Why we built this

If you have a legal problem in Agra, finding a lawyer usually means asking a relative who knows someone, or walking into the court compound and hoping for the best. National legal portals are no better — search “lawyer near me” and you will be shown advocates in Delhi or Noida who have never appeared before the Agra District Court.

We thought a city deserves better than that. So we built a directory covering Agra and Agra district only, listing advocates who actually practise here — at the District and Sessions Court, the Family Court, the Consumer Commission, the Labour Court, the MACT and the Sub-Registrar offices.

How a listing gets published

  1. 1

    The advocate contacts us

    We do not scrape listings from anywhere. An advocate joins only by sending us their details themselves, which means every profile here is consented to.

  2. 2

    We verify enrolment

    We check the Bar Council of Uttar Pradesh enrolment number, confirm the chamber address is in Agra, and speak to the advocate on the phone.

  3. 3

    The profile goes live

    Practice areas, case types, courts, languages, chamber address, consultation fee and phone number — published exactly as the advocate confirmed them.

What we are not

  • We are not a law firm and we do not give legal advice.
  • We do not take a commission from any fee you pay an advocate.
  • We do not sell your details — there is no sign-up and no lead form between you and the advocate.
  • We do not rank by payment. Nobody can pay for placement, and we publish no ratings at all — ordering is by experience and how complete a profile is.

A note on the Bar Council rules

Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules restricts advertising by advocates. Listings on this site are factual entries published at the request of the advocate concerned — name, enrolment, practice areas, address and contact details — and are not intended as advertisement, solicitation or an inducement. Nothing on this website should be treated as legal advice, and reading it creates no advocate–client relationship.
